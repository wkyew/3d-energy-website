About / 01

A new model for global energy education.

The 3D Energy Project pioneers a blended-learning framework built around shared digital coursework, cross-cultural collaboration, and the social weight of being in a room together.

Energy is a global problem with stubbornly local solutions — yet most energy education stays siloed within national curricula. 3D Energy assembles students and early-career researchers from four countries around a common microcredential on Smart Local Energy Systems, then brings them together each year for a physical summit hosted in rotation by the partners.

Four cohorts have completed over four different locations.

Approach / 02

Two halves. One experience.

A shared digital foundation, then the irreplaceable thing only a room can do — eye contact, accidents, friendship, the kind of collaboration that survives the flight home.

DIGITAL · 5 WEEKS
01Microcredential

Online microcredential

A self-paced digital programme delivered on Heriot-Watt's Open Learning platform — establishing the theoretical foundations of the 3D Energy concept and a shared vocabulary across four time zones.

  • Modular video lectures, self-paced
  • Gamified quizzes & interactive assignments
  • Weekly live Q&A with the four partner faculties
  • Cross-cohort discussion boards in three languages
PHYSICAL · 2 DAYS
02Summit

Annual physical summit

An in-person gathering hosted in rotation across Malaysia, Vietnam and China — the place where the microcredential's content meets weather, food, jet lag, and a few hundred handshakes.

  • Industry & academic keynote sessions
  • Cross-cultural team presentations
  • International Virtual Lab modules
  • Site visits, cultural exchange, and a social programme

Cohorts / 04

Five years. Four summits. One story.

Each cohort is hosted by a different partner — a deliberate choice, so that hospitality, climate, and culture are part of the curriculum rather than backdrop.

COHORT 01
December 2022 · Putrajaya

The inaugural summit, Malaysia

Hosted by Heriot-Watt University Malaysia

Twenty-six participants completed the digital microcredential and gathered in person for the first time — postponed from mid-2022 by COVID-19 restrictions, and all the more anticipated for it.

26 PARTICIPANTS·3 COUNTRIES·2 DAYS
COHORT 02
August 2023 · Ho Chi Minh City

Scaling the model, Vietnam

Hosted by HCMUT

The format settled. Larger cohort, formal industry partnerships, and the first iteration of the International Virtual Lab — connecting students across labs in three countries simultaneously.

32 PARTICIPANTS·4 COUNTRIES·2 DAYS
COHORT 03
June 2024 · Nanning

Inland routes, China

Hosted by Guangxi University

A site-visit-heavy summit centred on grid-edge infrastructure in southern China, paired with student-led research presentations and a public lecture co-hosted with the host institution.

28 PARTICIPANTS·4 COUNTRIES·2 DAYS + SITE VISITS
COHORT 04 · NOW
January 2026 · Putrajaya

The closing summit, Malaysia

Hosted by Heriot-Watt University Malaysia

A return to where it began. The fourth and final cohort of the funded programme — an alumni network of more than a hundred, and a curriculum ready to be handed on.
