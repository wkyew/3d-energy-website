About the project

A new model for global energy education.

The 3D Energy Project pioneers a blended learning framework that integrates digital coursework, cross-cultural collaboration, and hands-on experiential learning.

Energy is a global problem with local solutions — yet most energy education remains siloed within national curricula. The 3D Energy Project brings together students and early-career researchers from four countries to learn about Smart Local Energy Systems through a shared online microcredential, capped by an annual physical summit hosted in rotation across partner countries.

Three cohorts — Malaysia, Vietnam, China — have already graduated. A fourth cohort returned to Putrajaya in January 2026, completing the five-year journey.

How it works

Two components, one immersive experience.

DIGITAL
01

Online Microcredential

A 5-week self-paced digital programme delivered on Heriot-Watt's Open Learning platform — establishing the theoretical foundations of the 3D Energy concept.

  • Modular video lectures, self-paced
  • Gamified quizzes & interactive tasks
  • Weekly live Q&A with instructors
  • Cross-cohort discussion boards
PHYSICAL
02

Physical Summit

A 2-day in-person summit hosted in rotation across Malaysia, Vietnam, and China — where students apply their learning, network, and engage with industry.

  • Industry keynote sessions
  • Cross-cultural team presentations
  • International Virtual Lab modules
  • Site visits & cultural exchange
D
PILLAR / 01

Decarbonisation.

Reducing carbon emissions across the energy system through clean generation, storage, and grid integration.

D
PILLAR / 02

Decentralisation.

Empowering communities with smart local energy systems, distributed assets, and peer-to-peer energy markets.
